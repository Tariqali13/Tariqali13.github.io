ToolPilot AI Uploader – Privacy Policy

ToolPilot AI Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the ToolPilot AI YouTube channel (youtube.com/@ToolPilotAITools).

What it does

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and schedules, manage playlists, and post comments on the ToolPilot AI channel.

What data it uses

It only accesses the owner's own YouTube channel. The sign-in key is stored on the owner's own computer and is never sent anywhere else.

What it does not do

It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not access anyone else's account.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel through youtube.com/@ToolPilotAITools.
